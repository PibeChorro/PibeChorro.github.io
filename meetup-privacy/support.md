## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create an event?
Tap the **CREATE EVENT** button on the Events page to start creating a new event. You can set the details first and then select who to invite. 
You can also pick your friends first and fill in the details afterwards from the friends page.

### How do I accept an event invitation?
Open the event from your Events page and tap **Accept**. Once accepted, you will be added to the event's group chat automatically.

### How do I delete my account?
You can request account deletion directly in the app under **Settings → Delete Account**. All your personal data will be removed.

### I'm not receiving notifications. What can I do?
Make sure you have granted DASH permission to send notifications in your phone's system settings under **Settings → DASH → Notifications**.

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login screen, tap **Forgot password?** and follow the instructions to reset your password.
